**Meeting Minutes - Product Team Weekly Sync**
**Date:** January 15, 2024
**Attendees:** Sarah (Product Lead), Mike (Engineering), Lisa (Design)

**Agenda:**
1. Product launch review
2. Customer feedback discussion
3. Q1 roadmap planning

**Discussion Points:**

**Product Launch Review:**
- Successfully launched the new dashboard feature on January 10th
- Received overwhelmingly positive feedback from beta users

- Performance metrics showing 40% improvement in user engagement

**Customer Feedback:**

- Top request: mobile app support (mentioned by 60% of respondents)

**Q1 Roadmap:**

- Priority 3: Implement real-time collaboration features
- Target: Launch mobile app beta to 500 users by March 1st
- Sarah to present roadmap to board next week

**Action Items:**
- Mike: Fix database query performance issues by Jan 20
- Lisa: Create new onboarding flow mockups by Jan 18
- Sarah: Schedule mobile app development kickoff meeting
- All: Review and approve Q1 budget allocation by Jan 25
